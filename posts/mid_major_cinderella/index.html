Did NIL and paying players kill the mid-major cinderella?

NCAAMB
NCAA Tournament
Cinderella
Author

Ethan Warren

Published

April 24, 2026

The NIL era continues to create storylines that must sound made up at times, but one of the most interesting debates since the 2021 court case is if bigger schools being able to poach the best from lower power conferences and mid-major schools. With this idea it almost entirely created the idea that money would wipe out any chances of a mid-major from making a run in the NCAA Tournament. While this happens to be a question that we can easily wash away with the FAU and Princeton Final four and Sweet 16 runs in 2021. Net rating gives us a pretty good idea of how well a team performs over the course of a season, and how they should perform during March.

We can see that over the course of NIL and its rapid and ever changing landscape, that mid-majors over the course of time have gotten worse following the 2021 court case. But when looking at an era by era basis, we see that the one benefactor of the NIL era, is naturally gonna be the biggest schools. We see that since 2021 there has been a bigger spread towards both sides, which agrees with the sense of the biggest schools picking up the best players. What it never accounted for was unseen players in lower divisions like D2 being more valuable for schools of smaller scale. One instance we can see over the course of time is Iowa’s current head coach Ben McCollum and star player Bennett Stirtz, who both stepped up from Division II Northwest Missouri State and then made the tournament with the Drake Bulldogs, before ending up making a run with Iowa this March.

Another way that NIL has adjusted the building of teams is based on how floor time is distributed, the age old story of next man up caused teams to be built of every player on the team to contribute to major minutes during the game. The last of an aging era was No.16 seeded Siena who went toe to toe with No.1 seeded Duke with only five players WHO ALL PLAYED 40 MINUTES. Outside of that we continue to see star players carry a team while a bunch of other players share insignificant time over the course of a game.
